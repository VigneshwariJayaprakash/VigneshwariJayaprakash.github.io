About

My Journey

I began my career in 2013 at Infosys, where I spent six years embedded with BNSF Railway — one of North America's largest freight networks — progressing from Software Engineer to Data Scientist while shipping production ML and data systems for fraud detection, predictive maintenance, and freight operations analytics.

That tenure gave me deep rail & freight domain context: locomotive telemetry, revenue accounting workflows, regulatory reporting, intermodal logistics, and the operational rhythms of a Class I railroad. I'm now returning to that domain with a sharper toolkit — Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, and cloud-native ML.

After relocating to the United States, I invested in 1,200+ hours of self-directed upskilling in Generative AI, LLMs, RAG architectures, transformer internals, and cloud-native ML — building hands-on expertise in PyTorch, AWS, Docker, and vector databases before enrolling at ASU.

I'm completing an M.S. in Data Science (Computing & Decision Analytics) at Arizona State University with a perfect 4.0 GPA. I was also a Gold Medalist during my undergraduate studies at Anna University.

Most recently, I served as a Data Scientist Intern at the NM Department of IT — architecting a first-of-its-kind GenAI conversational analytics platform using LangGraph + RAG, and deploying ensemble anomaly detection models achieving 85% threat detection accuracy at statewide scale.
